ABOUT EXCHANGR

A deterministic exchange simulator with portable accounts.

Exchangr is designed to feel like a modern crypto trading platform while remaining completely static. It does not call an exchange API and it never moves real money.

Market clock

Prices are derived from Unix time. 9 AM–7 PM is the stronger session, 8 PM–midnight trends weaker, and the remaining overnight hours are comparatively stable. Small seeded variations keep each asset distinct.

Annual reset

At each new calendar year, the simulated pricing formula applies a 97% reset before the new year's gradual growth resumes. Historical charts apply the same rule, so past points remain reproducible.

Compact exchange keys

Your Exchangr key stores only your name, asset quantities, cost basis, and a short activity list. Price history, charts, net change, highs, and lows are recalculated from time instead of being embedded in the key.

Ledger compatibility

The trade ticket understands LEDGER1 tokens. When you buy, it deducts from the selected Ledger account. When you cash out, it credits the selected account after a 0.25% simulated fee and emits a fresh LEDGER1 token.
